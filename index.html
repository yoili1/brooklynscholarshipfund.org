How it works

Three steps from your gift to your tax return.

  1. From January 1, 2027

    Give in cash

    By check, card or bank transfer, marked as a Section 25F contribution. Stock and other property don’t qualify.

  2. By January 31

    Get your donor number

    We send a written statement with your total for the year and your donor number, and report it to the IRS.

  3. At tax time

    Claim the credit

    Enter your gift and donor number on IRS Form 8525 with your federal return.

Questions

Before you give.

What if I owe less federal tax than I gave?

The credit can only reduce tax you owe, so you won’t get a refund of the difference. Whatever you can’t use carries forward for up to 5 years.

Can I give more than $1,700?

Yes. Only the first $1,700 per person counts for the credit. The rest can be a regular charitable gift, deductible if you itemize.

Can I deduct the gift as well?

No. A gift you claim for the credit can’t also be deducted as a charitable contribution.

Can my business give?

No. Only individuals can claim the credit.

Can I choose which student gets my gift?

No. The law doesn’t allow donors to direct a gift to a particular student. Our committee awards every scholarship.

Is Brooklyn Scholarship Fund approved?

We’re a 501(c)(3) public charity and are applying for New York’s list of scholarship granting organizations, which the state sends to the IRS. We’ll post here as soon as we’re listed.
